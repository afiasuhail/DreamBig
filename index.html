Afia Suhail, Founder

Hello, I’m Afia Suhail, a student from Bhopal, India. I started DreamBig Education because I know that a good education can change everything for a person, yet I saw too many brilliant students without opportunities. That's why I’m working towards breaking financial barriers and making guidance to professional education more accessible. My dream is to spread educational opportunities throughout India, starting with my hometown.

Our journey, one year at a time.

What started as a small, informal effort in 2022 has grown into a foundation with real, measurable impact. Names of students are shortened to protect privacy.

2022
  • Informal workshops & mentorship for women's education in Bhopal.
  • Gave away old books and an iPad.
  • Realised: students needed funds, not just goodwill.
2023
  • Began collecting funds from family & friends for scholarships.
  • A neighbor's spare room became our first library.
  • Supported 9 students — Medical, Law, Engineering.
$1,890 collected 1 laptop + projector given 9 students supported monetarily
2024
  • Opened the DreamBig Education center in Bhopal.
  • Ran Arduino & HTML workshops.
  • Supported 16 students with tuition & hostel fees.
$3,537 collected 2 laptops given 16 students supported monetarily
2025
  • Expanded to villages near Bhopal.
  • Added volunteers to grow our reach.
  • Afia launched CodeByte, our coding app for students.
  • Supported 14 students with tuition fees.
$2,915 collected 4 laptops given 14 students supported monetarily
2026
  • Held 3 events on women's empowerment & education.
  • Supported more students (count pending).
